# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

A one-page front page summing up 23 November 2026's world news in a single
image: 31 stories, from the AI rout that hit Asian markets to the UN call to
regulate AI before it is too late, and a US lead over China at a record low.

## The day

- **AI ROUT HITS ASIA** — Kospi −3.3%, closing 4,708.54 and shedding 161
  points (intraday −5.8%). Samsung −3.1%, SK Hynix −2.5%.
- **GUTERRES: REGULATE AI BEFORE IT'S TOO LATE** — Bridgetown summit,
  Barbados: 150 delegates back a risk framework. A blueprint, not a treaty.
- **US LEAD OVER CHINA AT RECORD LOW** — Asia Power Index 2026: US 78.9,
  China 73.6, the narrowest gap (5.3) since 2018. US diplomatic influence
  −6 to 76.7; India third at 40.5.

Plus 10 cards, a world map with 13 numbered pins, an 18-item "Also on
Monday" list, and a DATE CHECK box listing what was *not* 23 November news.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 front page (drawn with PIL in `draw.py`)
- `main.py` — stdlib-only runner: validates the PNG and copies it to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
- `draw.py` — the drawing script that produced `entry.png`

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py   # writes $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png
```
